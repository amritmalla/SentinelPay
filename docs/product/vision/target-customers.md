# Target Customers

# Who SentinelPay Serves

SentinelPay is designed for organizations that process digital payments at scale and require greater intelligence, resilience, and operational visibility than traditional payment integrations provide.

Rather than replacing payment providers, SentinelPay becomes the decision layer that helps businesses optimize every payment.

---

# Primary Customer Segments

## E-Commerce Platforms

Large online retailers processing thousands or millions of transactions daily.

### Business Challenges

* Payment failures reduce revenue.
* Fraud increases operational costs.
* Single-provider dependency creates outages.
* Limited insight into payment performance.

### How SentinelPay Helps

* Intelligent payment routing
* Fraud prevention
* Automatic provider failover
* Real-time payment analytics
* Processing cost optimization
