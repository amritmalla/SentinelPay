# User Personas

# Who Uses SentinelPay

While organizations purchase SentinelPay, individual teams interact with the platform in different ways.

Each persona has distinct responsibilities, goals, and workflows.

---

# Merchant Administrator

## Responsibilities

Owns the organization's payment configuration.

### Goals

* Configure payment providers
* Manage payment policies
* Improve payment success rates
* Reduce operational costs

### Uses SentinelPay To

* Configure provider priorities
* Create routing rules
* Manage merchant settings
* View payment analytics

### Success Looks Like

More successful payments with lower operational effort.

---

# Risk Analyst

## Responsibilities

Protects the organization from fraudulent transactions.

### Goals

* Detect fraud
* Reduce false positives
* Investigate suspicious activity
* Improve fraud policies

### Uses SentinelPay To

* Review flagged transactions
* Create fraud rules
* Analyze fraud trends
* Investigate customer behavior

### Success Looks Like

Fraud is reduced while legitimate customers experience minimal friction.

---

# Payment Operations Specialist

## Responsibilities

Ensures payment systems operate efficiently.

### Goals

* Monitor payment flows
* Reduce payment failures
* Improve approval rates
* Resolve payment incidents

### Uses SentinelPay To

* Monitor provider health
* Replay failed transactions
* Analyze payment failures
* Trigger retries

### Success Looks Like

Stable payment operations with consistently high authorization rates.

---

# Platform Administrator

## Responsibilities

Manages the SentinelPay platform.

### Goals

* Manage organizations
* Configure tenants
* Control platform access
* Monitor overall platform health

### Uses SentinelPay To

* Manage organizations
* Configure authentication
* Manage roles
* Configure global policies

### Success Looks Like

A secure, scalable, and well-governed platform.

---

# Platform Engineer / SRE

## Responsibilities

Maintains platform reliability.

### Goals

* Detect incidents
* Reduce downtime
* Improve observability
* Optimize system performance

### Uses SentinelPay To

* View distributed traces
* Monitor latency
* Analyze Kafka consumers
* Investigate service failures
* Review infrastructure metrics

### Success Looks Like

Highly available payment infrastructure with rapid incident resolution.

---

# Customer Support Specialist

## Responsibilities

Assists customers experiencing payment issues.

### Goals

* Explain payment outcomes
* Resolve customer complaints
* Investigate failed transactions
* Escalate suspicious activity

### Uses SentinelPay To

* Search payment history
* View decision timelines
* Understand approval or rejection reasons
* Track transaction lifecycle

### Success Looks Like

Customers receive quick, accurate answers with minimal escalations.

---

# Executive Dashboard User

## Responsibilities

Monitors business performance.

### Goals

* Track revenue
* Measure payment success
* Understand fraud trends
* Evaluate provider performance

### Uses SentinelPay To

* View business dashboards
* Analyze KPIs
* Monitor cost savings
* Review platform health summaries

### Success Looks Like

Confident, data-driven strategic decisions based on payment intelligence.

# Persona Interaction Map

| Persona                       | Primary Domain        | Key Objectives                   |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Merchant Administrator        | Payment Configuration | Routing, providers, policies     |
| Risk Analyst                  | Risk Intelligence     | Fraud detection, investigations  |
| Payment Operations Specialist | Payment Operations    | Reliability, approval rates      |
| Platform Administrator        | Administration        | Organizations, users, governance |
| Platform Engineer / SRE       | Platform Operations   | Observability, resilience        |
| Customer Support Specialist   | Customer Service      | Payment investigations           |
| Executive Dashboard User      | Business Intelligence | KPIs, trends, strategic insights |

# Shared Goal

Although each persona has different responsibilities, they all depend on the same outcome:

> **Making every payment smarter through intelligent, explainable, and resilient decision-making.**
